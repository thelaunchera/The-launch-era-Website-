Automation · Quick Terms

Clear terms for the automation you choose.

These terms apply to The Launch Era one-time Automation setup for residential cleaning businesses. You choose either Instagram Automation with ManyChat or Website Automation with Make.

Important: Payment starts onboarding. Build work begins after these terms are accepted, the shared Automation Intake is complete, and the access/information needed for your selected path is available.

1 · Choose one automation path

Your checkout and intake identify the service being purchased: Instagram — ManyChat or Website — Make. The two paths have different prices and different technical requirements.

2 · Instagram Automation scope

Instagram Automation is a one-time ManyChat setup around one agreed Instagram DM use case.

Can include1 agreed DM flow · agreed keyword/entry triggers · message sequence · tags/routing · CTA or booking handoff · testing · 1 correction/testing round.

3 · Website Automation scope

Website Automation is a one-time Make setup around one agreed website lead or quote workflow.

Can include1 agreed website form/lead source · customer confirmation · lead routing/status step · one agreed follow-up or next-step action · testing · 1 correction/testing round.

4 · What is not included

Unless separately agreed in writing, the setup does not include a website redesign, full CRM build, unlimited forms or flows, ongoing customer service, ongoing social media management, custom software development, paid third-party platform fees, or unlimited revisions.

5 · Access + security

Use role-based access, collaborator invitations or the platform’s secure connection process when access is needed. Never send passwords, recovery codes or verification codes by email or through the intake.

6 · Timing

The production window begins after the shared intake, required access, links and workflow information are complete. Missing access, approvals or information may move delivery timing.

7 · Third-party platform limitations

Instagram Automation relies on Instagram/Meta and ManyChat. Website Automation may rely on Make and the client’s website, form, email, CRM or other agreed services. Third-party features, permissions, policies and availability can change, and The Launch Era cannot guarantee uninterrupted third-party functionality.

8 · Results

The Launch Era does not guarantee leads, bookings, response rates, revenue or other business outcomes. The setup is designed to make one agreed part of the inquiry and follow-up process clearer and easier to manage.

9 · Acceptance

By purchasing an Automation setup and submitting the shared Automation Intake, you confirm that you reviewed and accept these terms for the selected automation path.

What happens next

Complete the shared Automation Intake and select the same path you purchased. Instagram clients receive ManyChat connection steps. Website clients receive Make/website workflow connection steps.
