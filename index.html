Where good inquiries get lost

More inquiries do not fix a broken follow-up system.

A cleaning business can get attention and still lose good clients when the next step is unclear, replies are delayed or everything lives in different places.

01 · CLARITY

People see your business, but the next step is not obvious.

Potential clients should quickly understand what you offer, why they should trust you and how to move forward.

02 · RESPONSE

03 · FOLLOW-UP
