Questions

What to know before you buy.

Do the monthly content plans include Reels?

No. Starter, Growth and Pro include static feed posts, carousels, Stories, captions and hashtag sets. Reels are not included.

Do you post or manage my social media for me?

No. Content subscriptions cover strategy, writing and design. You receive finished, ready-to-post content. Reels, posting, scheduling, community management and paid ads are not included. Approved scheduling/posting support can be handled separately through VA Support when it fits that service scope.

Which package should I choose?

Starter is the smallest monthly content cycle, Growth gives you more consistency and campaign range, and Pro is the deepest monthly content system. Growth is the most balanced option for businesses that want consistent content without moving into the largest plan.

Is My Cleaning Hub required?

No. My Cleaning Hub is separate. It is available when you want a simpler place to organize leads, clients, quotes, invoices and your workflow.

Do you guarantee leads or bookings?

No. The Launch Era does not promise a specific number of leads or booked clients. We improve the trust, follow-up and organization around the opportunities your business receives so fewer good inquiries fall through the cracks.
