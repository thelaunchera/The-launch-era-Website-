No. We build the page for you. You send your business details through the form after purchase, and receive a link to share.

The app includes a basic booking and quote-request flow. This service adds your own public-facing page with your business presentation and both paths connected for you.

Can customers request a quote instead?

Yes. Work that needs custom pricing has its own quote-request path. You review the details before setting a price.

Complete the form from your purchase confirmation. Once the information and materials are complete, we build and test the page within 3 business days.

What do I pay after setup?

The page setup is $39.99 once. It includes 60 days of app access from activation. App access and connected booking features then require the $5.99/month subscription.

Can we work in Spanish?

Yes. The form, setup support and page copy can be handled in English or Spanish.
