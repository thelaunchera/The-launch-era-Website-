Before you order

Clear answers about the Booking Page.

Do I need a website already?

No. The Booking Page is specifically useful if you do not have a full website yet or if your current page does not give clients a clear booking path.

Can clients request a quote instead of booking?

Yes. Bookable services and quote requests have separate next steps so custom jobs do not get forced into a fixed-price booking flow.

Does the Booking Page replace the Cleaning App?

No. The Booking Page is client-facing. The Cleaning App is a separate workspace for the operational side of your business. Your Booking Page includes 2 months of app access.

Can we work in Spanish?

Yes. Onboarding and support are available in English and Spanish.
