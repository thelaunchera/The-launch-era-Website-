BEFORE YOU START

A few things you may be wondering.

Do I have to move everything over at once?

No. Start with one client and their next job. Add your other clients and services when you are ready. Sharing a booking link does require setting your services and availability first.

Does the Web App already include a booking link?

Yes. The Web App includes public booking and quote-request links. Booking Flow Setup is the separate service for a customized Booking Page plus client confirmation, owner alert, Private Lead Tracker and smart follow-up. It is currently $49.99 one-time (regular price $99).

Can my customers use a different language?

Yes. The web app and customer-facing flows support English, Spanish, French and Haitian Creole. Customer email language can be set separately from your web app language.

Does it collect my customers’ payments?

The web app records payments. You receive the money through your chosen method and confirm it in the web app.

Is this downloaded from the App Store?

No. It is a browser-based web app. Open it in your browser, add it to your Home Screen, and it will open and work like an app on your device.

What happens after the free period?

You can continue for $5.99/month if you choose. The Cleaning Web App subscription is separate from Booking Flow Setup.
