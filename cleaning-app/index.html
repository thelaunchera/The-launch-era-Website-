BEFORE YOU START

A few things you may be wondering.

Do I have to move everything over at once?

No. Start with one client and their next job. Add your other clients and services when you are ready. Sharing a booking link does require setting your services and availability first.

Does the app already include a booking link?

Yes. The app includes public booking and quote-request links. The separate $24.99 service adds a customized page with your branding, services and those links connected for you.

Can my customers use a different language?

Yes. The app and customer-facing flows support English, Spanish, French and Haitian Creole. Customer email language can be set separately from your app language.

Does it collect my customers’ payments?

The app records payments. You receive the money through your chosen method and confirm it in the app.

What happens after the free period?

Continue for $5.99/month. The Booking Page package includes its own 60-day access period from activation; your account shows the end date.
