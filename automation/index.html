Will this make my business feel robotic?

No. We keep the experience human and automate only the predictable steps that make sense.

What if I already have a website form?

That is exactly what this service is built for. We do not replace your website. We improve what happens after someone submits your existing contact or quote form.

Are you building me a new website?

No. Website Automation is for businesses that already have a website. We connect and organize one existing lead flow so inquiries are easier to respond to and follow up with.

Automated Booking or Website Automation — which one do I need?

Choose Automated Booking + Follow-Up when you need the client booking experience plus lead tracking and follow-up. Choose Website Automation when you already have a website form and only need the steps after submission automated.

Do I need the Cleaning Web App for Website Automation?

No. Website Automation can work with your existing website as a separate service. The Cleaning Web App is not required.

What does the $59.99 cover?

The $59.99 covers The Launch Era’s one-time Website Automation setup. Your website plan, hosting, domain and any optional third-party tools remain under your business account. If a paid app, plugin, premium integration or plan upgrade is needed, we’ll confirm it with you before setup.

Is this ongoing management?

No. This is a one-time setup. Additional support can be added separately if you need it later.
