Quick questions

Before you open the Hub.

Do customers need a My Cleaning Hub account?

No. They open your public booking link, choose the service and time, enter their information and confirm.

Can two customers book the same time?

The Hub checks availability again before confirming and accounts for existing bookings and calendar appointments.

Do I need Stripe?

No. Booking works without online payment. If Stripe is connected, it becomes the main secure payment option. You can also configure Zelle or accept cash on spot.

Will repeat customers create duplicates?

The Hub matches customers using normalized email or phone so one client can have multiple bookings.

What does my customer see?

Your customer sees your business booking page with your business information and the options you make available for quote requests or bookings.

Is My Cleaning Hub required to buy another The Launch Era service?

No. My Cleaning Hub is a separate, optional product.
