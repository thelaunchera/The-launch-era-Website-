RECOMMENDATIONS

Top Priorities

Small changes. A bigger impact. No rebrand needed.

1

Make “Request a Free Quote” the clearest next step.

Bio direction + one pinned conversion post + a dedicated quote Highlight. The visitor should never wonder what to do next.

2

Reorder Highlights around client questions.

Keep the family warmth, but move Services, Reviews, Our Work, FAQ and Quote information earlier.

3

Bring more real team, process and trust content toward the top of the feed.

The polished graphics are strong. More human proof will deepen trust and give Reels more natural reach potential.
