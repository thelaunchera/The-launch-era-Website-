THE LAUNCH ERACONTENT REVIEW
The feed is polished — now give the people more room

A stronger content rhythm.

The recent grid looks professional and credible, especially for commercial work. The risk is that too many polished graphics in a row can make the feed feel like a brochure. Maria already has the fix inside the account: real people, real work and real moments.

ProofShow it

Real job footage, before/after, commercial site, detail shots, reviews.

Maria, team members, family, behind-the-scenes, birthdays and community.

OfferConvert it

Service spotlight, free estimate, “what happens next,” and clear booking/quote prompts.

Instead of “less sentimental,” think better sequencing: proof → people → offer → repeat.

THE LAUNCH ERAVIEWS + DISCOVERY
For more views without becoming generic

Three Reel systems Maria can repeat.

The best reach system here is not “post more.” It is making original, recognizable formats the team can film on real jobs.

01

Maria notices this first

10–15 seconds. Show one detail most people miss in a home, office or post-construction space.

Hook: “Most people clean this. We check this first.”
02

Before they walk in

Fast visual transformation from “not ready” to “client-ready” using real job footage.

Hook: “This space had one hour before handoff.”
03

Arizona clean check

Use local context: dust, construction residue, desert windows, high-traffic commercial spaces.

Hook: “Arizona dust always leaves evidence here.”
01

Meet the team

One person, one sentence, one real moment. Avoid formal introductions; let personality do the work.

Question: “What makes a clean space feel finished to you?”
02

30+ years, one lesson

Maria speaks directly to camera. Short expertise makes the founder visible and memorable.

Hook: “After 30+ years, this is the mistake I still see.”
03

Real reference + real job

Pair a client quote with the actual project footage instead of using a review by itself.

Format: result → client words → team moment.
01

What happens after you ask for a quote

Show the exact next steps so people know what to expect before clicking.

Hook: “Requesting an estimate should not feel complicated.”
02

Residential vs. commercial

Explain which path is right for each client in under 20 seconds.

Hook: “Need your home cleaned? Do this. Managing a facility? Do this.”
03

One service, one proof, one CTA

Feature a service using real footage, one proof point and one clear action.

CTA: “Request a free estimate from the link in our bio.”

Why prioritize original footage: Meta said in January 2026 that 75% of Instagram recommendations in the U.S. were coming from original posts. That supports leaning harder into Maria's real team, real jobs and founder-led content.

THE LAUNCH ERA30-DAY FOCUS
Simple, realistic and focused

Maria's 30-day plan.

1

Profile + Highlights

  • Update the Name field to include Arizona.
  • Use the shorter bio direction.
  • Add Start Here + Free Quote highlights.
  • Move Services, Reviews and Our Work toward the front.

Goal for the week

A first-time visitor should know what Maria does, where she works, why she is trusted and what to do next without scrolling.

2

Pin the direction

  • Pin Start Here / Services.
  • Pin Why Arizona Trusts Maria's.
  • Pin Request a Free Quote.
  • Keep the rest of the feed free to be human, seasonal and alive.

Goal for the week

The top row handles clarity + conversion, so the rest of the account does not have to become sales-heavy.

3

Original trust content

  • Film one “Maria notices this first” Reel.
  • Film one team/process Reel.
  • Post one real reference paired with job footage.
  • Start the “30+ years, one lesson” series.

Goal for the week

Make the feed feel more like a living company with people and expertise — not only beautiful graphics.

4

Conversion + learning

  • Publish “What happens after you request a quote.”
  • Use one clear CTA in stories for 3–4 days.
  • Track profile visits, website taps, DMs and quote requests.
  • Keep the formats that bring the right questions — not only the most likes.

Goal for the week

Judge content by whether it creates qualified interest and easier conversations, not vanity metrics alone.
