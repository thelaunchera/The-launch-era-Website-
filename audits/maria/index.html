THE LAUNCH ERAVIEWS + DISCOVERY
For more views without becoming generic

Three Reel systems Maria can repeat.

The reach system is not “post more.” It is making original, recognizable formats the team can film on real jobs.

01

Maria notices this first

Show one detail most people miss in a home, office or post-construction space.

Hook: “Most people clean this. We check this first.”
02

Before they walk in

Fast transformation from “not ready” to “client-ready” using real footage.

Hook: “This space had one hour before handoff.”
03

Arizona clean check

Use local context: dust, construction residue, desert windows and commercial traffic.

Hook: “Arizona dust always leaves evidence here.”
01

Meet the team

One person, one sentence, one real moment. Let personality do the work.

Question: “What makes a space feel finished to you?”
02

30+ years, one lesson

Maria speaks directly to camera and turns experience into a repeatable series.

Hook: “After 30+ years, this is the mistake I still see.”
03

Real reference + real job

Pair a client quote with actual project footage instead of using a review by itself.

Format: result → client words → team moment.
01

What happens after a quote request

Show the exact next steps so people know what to expect before clicking.

Hook: “Requesting an estimate should not feel complicated.”
02

Residential vs. commercial

Explain which path is right for each client in under 20 seconds.

Hook: “Need your home cleaned? Do this. Managing a facility? Do this.”
03

One service, one proof, one CTA

Feature a service with real footage, one proof point and one clear action.

CTA: “Request a free estimate from the link in our bio.”

Why lean into original footage: Meta said in January 2026 that 75% of Instagram recommendations in the U.S. were coming from original posts.

3

Original Trust Content

  • Film one “Maria notices this first” Reel.
  • Film one team/process Reel.

Goal

  • Publish “What happens after you request a quote.”

Judge content by whether it creates useful conversations and quote requests — not likes alone.
