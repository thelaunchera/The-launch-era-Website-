The Launch Era30-Day Focus
Simple, realistic and focused

30-Day Focus

Week 1 · Profile + Highlights

  • Refine the bio so service + trust + quote CTA read in that order.
  • Add a “Start Here” highlight.
  • Move Services, Reviews, Our Work and Free Quote closer to the front.
  • Keep Team & Family visible - it is part of the trust story.
✓ Result: a new visitor understands the account faster.

Week 2 · Pinned Content

  • Pin “Start Here / Our Services.”
  • Pin “Why Arizona Trusts Maria's.”
  • Pin “Request a Free Quote.”
  • Use short, large, readable copy and real Maria's visuals.
✓ Result: the first row tells the conversion story.

Week 3 · Trust Content

  • Publish one real team/process Reel.
  • Publish one behind-the-scenes job Story sequence.
  • Publish one testimonial/reference post.
  • Use Arizona / city context naturally in the opening frame or caption.
✓ Result: more human proof for new visitors.

Week 4 · Conversion

  • Publish one quote-focused Reel, carousel or Story sequence.
  • Explain what information a client should send.
  • Show what happens after the estimate request.
  • Keep the CTA friendly: “Request a Free Quote.”
✓ Result: less uncertainty before someone reaches out.
