How your Content project works.

This page is your quick guide for assets, draft review, revisions and final delivery.

1 · Before we start

Upload your logo, brand colors/fonts if you have them, approved business photos, service list, service area, booking link, current offers and important wording/preferences into your private client folder under 01 — BRAND ASSETS + PHOTOS.

2 · Draft review

When your first batch is ready, you’ll receive it in 02 — DRAFTS — CLIENT REVIEW. Please review the full batch before sending changes.

3 · How to send changes

Send one consolidated list of edits for the full batch. Reference the file/post number and the exact change.

Example
Post 02 — Slide 1: change “busy families” to “busy homeowners.”
Post 04 — Caption: remove the word “luxury.”

4 · Included revision round

Your Monthly Content service includes 1 consolidated revision round. Send one list of reasonable edits for the full monthly batch. A completely new concept, new service/offer, full redesign, replacement post or new creative direction is outside the included revision round.

5 · Review window

Please send feedback within 3 business days when possible. Late feedback may move the final delivery date.

6 · Final approval

Before approving, check business spelling, services, service area, offers, booking information and any business-specific claims.

7 · Final delivery

Approved files go into 04 — FINAL DELIVERY. Captions, hashtags, CTA notes and posting order go into 05 — CAPTIONS + POSTING PLAN.

8 · Communication

Keep project feedback in the agreed review channel instead of splitting changes across DMs, texts and email.

The goal is to make this part of your business easier to manage — not add another complicated process.
